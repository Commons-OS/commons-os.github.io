Every living system is unique. Before you can find the right patterns, you must understand the forces at play in your specific situation. The Commons Suit is a conversational partner designed to help you do just that.

Meet the Commons Suit Lite

This is not a search engine. It’s an empathetic listener. Start a conversation about your challenge, and the Commons Suit will help you articulate your context, discover underlying tensions, and find patterns that resonate with your reality.

How It Works

A simple, four-step dialogue to move from ambiguity to clarity.

1

Share Your Challenge

Start a conversation about what you’re working on—transforming your organization, building a community, planning a city project.

2

Discover Your Context

Through empathetic dialogue, the suit builds understanding of your unique situation: your domain, constraints, culture, and goals.

3

Receive Adapted Patterns

Based on your context, the suit suggests patterns from the library, adapted to your reality and linked to real-world Lighthouses.

4

Iterate and Evolve

Come back as your situation changes. The Commons Suit is a companion for your journey, not a one-time consultation.
